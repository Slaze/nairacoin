Two kinds of “wallet”

1. Product IOU (live today in Lvfe)
Your NCN balance for staking and bidding lives in the Lvfe save pack. You can buy into it with normal payment rails. It is real for gameplay. It is not a confirmed on-chain address you can spend on a public daemon yet.

2. On-chain Nairacoin (not public-live yet)
CryptoNote-style addresses (often starting with f) need keys and a running nairacoind. We will not show a fake spendable mainnet balance here.

Demo faucets inside games stay labelled demo. Purchased NCN for play is separate from demo credit.
